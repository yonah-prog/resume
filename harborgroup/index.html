About HGI

Harbor Group International (HGI) is a leading global real estate investment and management firm. With $21 billion1 in real estate investment properties, the firm invests in and manages diversified property portfolios including office, retail, and multifamily properties.

With over 40 years of experience in the industry and approximately 1,600 employees worldwide, HGI continues to look for real estate investment opportunities.
